Customer Email Support Sample

Example of how I would professionally handle a damaged item and replacement request.

Customer Inquiry

Subject: Damaged Item Received – Order #10458

Hi,

I received my order today, but unfortunately the item arrived damaged. The packaging was also slightly crushed when it was delivered.

I need the item as soon as possible. Can you please arrange a replacement?

Thank you,
Sarah

My Response

Subject: Re: Damaged Item Received – Order #10458

Hi Sarah,

I'm sorry to hear that your order arrived damaged. I understand how frustrating this can be, especially when you were expecting to use the item right away.

I'd be happy to assist you with the replacement. To help us verify the damage and process your request, could you please send a clear photo of the damaged item and its packaging?

Once we receive the photos, I'll review the details and proceed with the appropriate replacement process. I'll also keep you updated on the status so you know what to expect next.

Thank you for your patience, and I apologize for the inconvenience.

Best regards,
Jan Triston Abella
